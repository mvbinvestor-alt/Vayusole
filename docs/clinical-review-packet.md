## What we are and are not asking

We are **not** asking you to endorse reflexology, or to comment on whether it
works. The app already states that no systematic review has demonstrated
reflexology treats a specific medical condition, and grades nothing above C.

We are asking one narrow question:

> **Is the safety guidance below adequate to keep a member of the public from
> being physically harmed by following these instructions at home?**

Specifically: are the contraindications correct, complete, and stated strongly
enough? Please strike, amend, or add anything.

---

## What the app does

- Presents 10 self-care protocols of 4–7 minutes each
- Each names 3 foot pressure points, a sequence of manual steps, and a timer
- Each carries one safety line and an evidence grade (C or D only)
- No diagnosis, no measurement, no sensors, no medication advice
- Users manually press their own feet with thumbs, or use a ball/roller

**Physical risk surface:** self-applied manual pressure to the foot, plus four
home remedies involving warm water soaks, essential oils, and topical products.

## Section 2 — Global contraindications

Currently shown on the first-session acknowledgement screen and the Why tab.
Please confirm this list is complete and correctly worded.

Currently listed:

1. Pregnancy
2. Diabetes / diabetic neuropathy
3. Circulation problems
4. Recent injury or fracture
5. Infection
6. Open wound on the foot
7. Fever

**Specifically, please advise on:**

- [ ] **DVT / known or suspected blood clot.** Named in our README as needing
      review but not currently in the user-facing list. Should manual foot
      pressure be explicitly contraindicated here? We believe yes.
- [ ] **Peripheral arterial disease** — include separately from "circulation"?
- [ ] **Anticoagulant therapy** — does this warrant its own warning?
- [ ] **Lymphoedema / post-lymph-node-dissection** — relevant to the
      "Lymphatic" point work?
- [ ] **Recent surgery** — any window to specify?
- [ ] **Epilepsy, cardiac conditions, active cancer** — commonly listed by
      practitioner bodies. Warranted, or over-warning?
- [ ] **Pregnancy** — currently "check with a professional". Should specific
      points (Pelvic / Uterus, RF-12) be disabled entirely instead?
- [ ] **Children** — should there be a minimum age?
